About Me

I'm a Computer Science student at UC Berkeley, where I transferred from El Camino College as a Sharer Scholar. I got into programming by building things: my first real project was a Discord bot for a Minecraft community I founded, which grew to over 1,200 members. Now I'm building an interactive web experiment as a researcher in Prof. Bill Thompson's Computational Cognitive Science Lab and helping students work through CS 61A as a teaching intern. I'm most interested in full-stack development and building software people actually use.

More About Me

I learn best by building. Most of what I know started with a project I didn't fully know how to finish yet, and figuring it out along the way taught me to get comfortable with uncertainty. Teaching CS 61A has added another side to that. Explaining recursion to a student who's stuck means understanding it well enough to break it into small steps, and that habit carries over to how I approach my own code.

Outside of academics, I play on UC Berkeley's main Rocket League esports team and in volleyball intramurals.
